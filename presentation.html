Methodology: Controlled Data-Centric AI

Identical 3-Block CNN Topology (~320K parameters) • Zero architecture tricks

  • Tilt & Incline: cv2.warpAffine with reflection padding (±15° range).
  • Optical Defocus / Motion Blur: cv2.GaussianBlur with odd kernel sizes (3 to 9).
  • Illumination Variance: cv2.convertScaleAbs (α ∈ [0.6, 1.3], β ∈ [-35, 25]).
  • Semantic Directional Integrity: Horizontal flipping strictly prohibited to prevent sign inversion.
